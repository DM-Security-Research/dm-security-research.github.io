# Responsible disclosure and research ethics

DM Security Research follows an authorization-first approach.

- We test only assets that we own or have explicit permission to assess.
- We observe defined scope, rate limits, data-handling requirements, and program terms.
- We avoid disruptive testing against production systems and do not access or retain third-party personal data unless expressly authorized and necessary.
- We document findings with the minimum reproducible evidence required.
- We notify affected maintainers or follow their official disclosure channels where appropriate.
- We do not publicly reveal sensitive exploit details while a coordinated disclosure process is ongoing.
- We distinguish open research questions, reproduced behaviors, confirmed security issues, and independently validated impact.
