# Research methodology

DM Security Research is an independent research initiative based in Argentina, led by David Menendez.

## Scope of work

Research interests include source-code review, browser and JavaScript-engine behavior, application security, cryptographic workflows, and reverse engineering.

## Controlled validation process

1. Identify the precise component, version, trust boundary, and applicable authorization.
2. Develop a testable hypothesis using source review or a local laboratory.
3. Construct the smallest safe reproducible test that can confirm or falsify the hypothesis.
4. Record test conditions, expected behavior, observed behavior, and relevant limitations.
5. Distinguish suspected issues from confirmed findings and avoid claims of impact without evidence.
6. Follow the relevant coordinated disclosure procedure when a valid security issue is identified.

Private repositories and ongoing investigations are not published by default. Research summaries are only made public when they can be shared responsibly and without violating applicable program rules.

## Authorized-use statement

Testing is limited to systems, code, and environments owned by the researcher or explicitly authorized for assessment. DM Security Research does not claim affiliation with audited vendors or security programs unless separately documented.
