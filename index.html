About

Independent by design.

Based in Argentina, DM Security Research conducts independent technical investigations using isolated laboratories and a methodical process of hypothesis development, controlled testing, evidence collection, and verification. The initiative is not presented as a legally incorporated company.

Research areas

Areas of technical work

Application & source-code security

Security-oriented code review, logic flaws, trust boundaries, authorization controls, and defensive validation.

Browsers & runtime behavior

Analysis of JavaScript engines, WebKit-related components, isolation boundaries, and standards-compliant behavior.

Protocol & cryptographic workflows

Investigation of signing authorization, protocol implementation, message validation, and security invariants.

Reverse engineering & local laboratories

Static analysis, reproducible test harnesses, controlled proofs of concept, and structured technical documentation.

Technical portfolio

Research practice, with clear evidence boundaries.

Our research includes browser isolation, protocol validation, source-code auditing and controlled reverse engineering. The portfolio describes methods and subject areas—not confirmed vulnerabilities or vendor endorsements.

Research principles

Authorization comes first.

Our work is restricted to systems we own or are explicitly permitted to assess. We prioritize safe and reproducible validation, respect testing scope and program rules, protect sensitive information, and pursue coordinated responsible disclosure. Ongoing investigations and private repositories are not publicly disclosed without appropriate authorization.
